# Contract: Homepage Sections

The homepage at `/` exposes seven visitor-facing blocks in this order. This is the content contract. It is not an API.

## Hero

- Headline: "Turning real problems into useful digital products."
- Description: "QNR Labs is an independent digital studio building its own products and developing practical digital solutions for others."
- The hero also shows that the studio is founder-led.
- Primary action: "Explore Digital Labs", target the Digital Labs block on this page.
- Secondary action: "How We Build", target the Development Approach block.
- The hero does not call the studio an open-source identity.

## Development Approach

- Heading: "Thoughtful development, from idea to release."
- Stages, in order: Discover, Define, Design, Build, Validate, Deliver.
- Each stage uses the meaning in `SECTION-SUGGESTED.md`.
- The block states that the stages are a recommended framework, not a sequence every project must follow.
- This is the only block that lists those stages.

## Digital Labs

- Shows only records from the published-project rules in [data-model.md](../data-model.md).
- Account Switcher is shown with "100+ installs".
- Unresolved fields remain visible as `[TBD]` labels.
- Does not show an "Open source" statistic.
- Does not show the sample category, problem sentence, stack, or license as if they were confirmed.

## Services

- Heading: "Turning your ideas into practical digital solutions."
- Description: "From early concepts to working products, QNR Labs helps turn ideas and real-world challenges into practical digital solutions through thoughtful development and iterative experimentation."
- If the four example names are shown, the same block says they are not a confirmed offer.
- The block contains no price, client, testimonial, or contact form.
- The block is not the contact path.

## Open Source

- Says QNR publishes selected projects.
- Says that not every project is open source.
- Does not label Account Switcher as open source.

## Contact call to action

- The only block that invites the visitor to contact QNR directly.
- Shows Email and GitHub.
- An unknown address is shown as unresolved, not as a working link to a guessed destination.
- Contains no form and does not repeat the six stages or the four service names.

## Footer

- Unchanged from the current footer.
- Purpose line remains "Building useful software, tools, and digital solutions."
- Unresolved GitHub and Email labels remain unresolved.

## Page-level refusals

The homepage does not include About, FAQ, a team, a price, a client name, a testimonial, a contact form, or a public expansion of "QNR".
