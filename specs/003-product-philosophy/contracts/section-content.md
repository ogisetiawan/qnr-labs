## Stages (exactly four)

### 01 — Notice & Capture

- Find & make feel problems, frustrations, and things that could be better.
- Write down rough ideas without worrying about perfect solutions.

### 02 — Think & Validate

- Structure ideas using SMART and explore possible solutions.
- Evaluate feasibility, potential value, and practical limitations.

### 03 — Define

- Research, learn, and adapt relevant insights to the original goals
- Shape the solution and prioritize essential features for the first version.

### 04 — Create & Share

- Initialize the project, develop, test, and refine the product.
- Publish the finished product so others can benefit from it

## Visual structure (acceptance shape)

- Wide: four columns/stages in one row; connecting line behind indicators.
- Narrow: stacked or wrapped reading order 01→04; no horizontal page scroll; connector adapted or omitted.
